# Symptom v1.0 Switch Options

This folder tracks switch options, switch feel, source references, and datasheets for Symptom v1.0.

For the first sale batch, only Kailh switches are customer-facing. HanElectricity switches are tracked as a later low-cost test option and should not be offered until they have been built and tested.

## First Batch Switch Allocation

| Keyboard count | Switch option | Customer-facing feel | Status |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Kailh Pro Burgundy `CPG151101D221` | Linear, low sound, 50 +/- 10 gf, 1.7 +/- 0.6 mm pre-travel | Ready for Shopify option |
| 2 | Kailh Pro Purple `CPG151101D222` | Tactile, medium sound, 50 +/- 10 gf, 60 +/- 10 gf tactile force, 1.7 +/- 0.6 mm pre-travel | Ready for Shopify option |
| 1 | Kailh Brown regular, shop option `Brown-CPG151801S04` | Tactile, medium sound, 50 +/- 10 gf, 60 +/- 10 gf tactile force, 1.9 +/- 0.5 mm pre-travel | Ready for Shopify option |
